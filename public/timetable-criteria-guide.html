High school only (O Level, A Level, TVET, Special). Nursery and primary are not covered by these locked rules. Generate with Replace locked timetable before checking.

2. How generation works

1. Load Manage Course assignments → 2. Apply locked document rules → 3. Place combined groups first → 4. Copy the same slot onto every partner class → 5. Place remaining courses (morning first)
  1. Scope. These rules run only for high school tracks. Nursery and primary keep their own generators.
  2. Locked criteria. Alice’s Monday ban, named teacher windows, clinical hours, and combine groups are never dropped when a teacher looks overloaded.
  3. Blocks (consecutive hours). Weekly periods are split before placement:
    • 4 or 6 periods → doubles only (2+2 or 2+2+2)
    • 3, 5 or 7 periods → 2 together and 1 separate (5 → 2+2+1 = 3 teacher sessions)
    • 2 periods → two singles on different days
    • PE → last teaching period (15:00–15:40). If that cell is taken, the other lesson moves earlier. Never 13:40, never after 15:40.
    • Farming / Library and Clubs → after 15:40 only (before 17:30 night). Same teacher + same activity share one clock across classes.
  4. Combine. Word-file groups stay one subject + same teacher. Farming and Library and Clubs also combine when the teacher is the same (Farming with Farming, Library with Library). Different academic subjects never share a clock.
  5. Named teachers. Innocent, Eric (L3 SOD), Olivier, Varlette, Marguerite, Linea, Patience, Alice may only sit in their document windows.
  6. Everyone else. Normal placement, filling from morning periods first, then afternoon if morning is full.
  7. Maths & Physics prefer 07:00–12:00. ANP teaching prefers Tuesday–Thursday mornings.
  8. Clinical. S4/S5 ANP Tuesday 07:00–12:00 and S6 ANP Wednesday 07:00–16:00 stay free of ordinary lessons.
  9. Lock. Combined, PE last-hour, Farming/Library after-15:40, and windowed lessons are saved locked. A later generate will not move them unless you check Replace locked timetable.

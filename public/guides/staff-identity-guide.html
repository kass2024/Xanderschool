How to record staff identity and download it

From the staff member, accountant, Chief Accountant, or Head Teacher filling the form, to the Director of Finance downloading the identifications PDF. Wisdom School Musanze and its child schools.

Who does what Fill your own profile Non-Rwandan details Fill for another staff member Director of Finance download What the PDF contains

1. Who does what

PersonWhat they do
Each staff memberOpens My profile and fills their own identity, including the ID or passport upload.
Accountant, Chief Accountant, Head TeacherOpen Staff, click the person’s name, and fill the same identity form for them.
Director and Director of FinanceOn the staff list, choose the Wisdom school, tick people or search, then click IDs PDF. The file includes the uploaded ID or passport.

2. A staff member updates their own information

  1. Sign in, then open the profile menu at the top right and choose My profile.
  2. On the Identity card, choose Rwandan or Non-Rwandan.
  3. Fill the fields for that choice. Upload the ID or passport as a JPG, PNG, or PDF, no larger than 5 MB.
  4. Click Save identity. The page reloads when the save succeeds.
The national ID must be 16 digits. Phone and email are optional for Rwandan staff. The upload is required the first time.

3. Non-Rwandan staff: ID or passport

Choose Non-Rwandan. Fill every field below, choose ID or Passport, then upload that document.

4. Accountant, Chief Accountant, or Head Teacher fills it for a staff member

  1. Open Staff in the side menu.
  2. Click the staff member’s name. That opens the same Identity card.
  3. Fill the Rwandan or Non-Rwandan fields and upload the ID or passport.
  4. Click Save identity.

A staff member who is not one of those three posts can save only their own profile. They cannot change another person’s identity.

5. Director of Finance downloads the identifications

  1. Sign in as Director of Finance and open Staff.
  2. If the file should be for a child school, use Export from school and pick that campus. The note at the top names the school the export will use.
  3. Tick one person, tick several, or type in the search box. Search also matches national ID, passport number, and parent names.
  4. Click the dark IDs PDF button. The same choice is in the menu on the right as Identifications PDF.
If nobody is ticked, type a search first. The PDF then includes everyone that search shows. If the search is empty and nobody is ticked, the page asks you to choose staff before it downloads.

6. What the Director of Finance receives

Each person gets a page with the school letterhead and logo, the identity fields, and the uploaded photo. If the upload was a PDF, those pages are attached after the identity sheets, with the staff member’s name.

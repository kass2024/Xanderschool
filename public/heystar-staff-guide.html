WISDOM SCHOOL RWANDA

Staff face attendance on the HeyStar terminal — school ID 27

The screen should show the school name and logo and a live camera that is always ready. Do not tap Check-In / Check-Out. This machine is for staff faces only. Students use the Xander card scanner, not this camera.

1. Clock IN or OUT (every day)

  1. Walk up and look at the camera. Keep still for about one second. There is no direction button to press.
  2. Green light = your face was found. The screen shows your name and IN or OUT, and the terminal speaks it. Xander uses your staff shift for late/overtime, same as the web attendance scanner.
  3. Red light = not found. Do not keep retrying in a crowd. Ask an admin to register your face (section 2).
  4. The first successful look of the day is IN (only one IN). Wait 5 minutes, then later looks are OUT and replace the previous out time.

2. Register a staff face (admin, first time)

  1. On Xander web: add the staff member first (they must already exist).
  2. Add the staff member on Xander. Names auto-sync to this terminal when the school network is up. You can also tap Sync staff names to HeyStar in School Settings to force a refresh.
  3. On the terminal: tap the settings gear. Password: 123456.
  4. Open User Management. Find the staff name.
  5. Choose register / capture face. The person looks at the camera until it saves.
  6. Go back to the live camera screen and test: they should get a green light.

3. Lights

  • Green — staff found, clock saved to Xander.
  • Red — face not in this school’s list (or not enrolled yet).

4. School ID

This terminal fetches and clocks WISDOM SCHOOL RWANDA using school ID 27 (acronym WSR).

To point the same machine at another school later: enter that school’s ID in Xander School Settings, save, then Sync from the school LAN.

5. If something fails

  • Always red: face not registered, or staff was added on the web after the last sync. Sync again, then register the face.
  • Green but no report: check school Wi‑Fi. Clocks upload to schoolmis.xanderglobalacademy.com with school_id=27.
  • Wrong school name/logo: Sync again from School Settings while the PC can reach the terminal IP.
  • Settings: password 123456. Communication password for the LAN API is also 123456.
